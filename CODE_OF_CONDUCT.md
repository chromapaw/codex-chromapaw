# Code of conduct

ChromaPaw is committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent while discussing the technical impact of a change.
- Respect privacy, copyright, accessibility needs, and platform safety boundaries.
- Help newcomers reproduce problems without asking them to publish private images, logs, tokens, or runtime state.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized language, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information or artwork without permission.
- Pressuring contributors to bypass Codex, Windows, or macOS security protections.
- Repeated disruption, spam, or knowingly misleading platform-support claims.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release channels, and other spaces represented as part of the ChromaPaw project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary moderation requests, contact the maintainers in a repository discussion. For a confidential report, use GitHub's [private reporting form](https://github.com/chromapaw/codex-chromapaw/security/advisories/new) and begin the title with `Conduct report`. Do not include private source images, access tokens, or unrelated personal information.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove a participant when behavior threatens the community or project. Reports will be handled as privately as the platform permits, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.
